About Us

Helping students, educators, and job seekers build brighter futures through practical education, skill development, and recognized career-focused training programs.

Our Identity

Building Skilled Professionals. Empowering Future Educators.

CHRD Training Academy works under the Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala). CHRD Kerala is an ISO (9001:2015) certified Centre — a center of highly qualified teachers, trainers, and educational strategists.

Established with a vision to make high-quality, job-oriented education accessible across Kerala, we serve students, educators, professionals, and institutions by developing practical learning programs that combine academic excellence with workforce needs.

At CHRD, we believe education should not only provide certificates but also create real opportunities, build self-confidence, and prepare individuals for long-term success in life and career.

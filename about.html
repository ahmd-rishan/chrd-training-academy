About Us

Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala) is an ISO 9001:2015 certified hub of qualified educators, professional trainers, and industry mentors dedicated to empowering learners through practical skill mastery.

Our Institution Story

Empowering Careers Through Accessible & Quality Skill Education

CHRD Training Academy operates under the esteemed Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala). Recognized as the Best Skill Development Academy in Malappuram, our institution was founded with a clear directive: to transform job seekers, educators, students, and working professionals into highly competent workforce members.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certification reflects our uncompromising commitment to structured educational governance, practical curriculum design, and continuous student assessment. We combine academic rigor with live hands-on practice across Early Childhood Montessori Teacher Training, Digital Marketing, Hospital Administration, Legal Assistant training, and Office Administration.

At CHRD, education extends far beyond awarding certificates. We foster an encouraging, learner-centered environment where every individual builds genuine self-confidence, masters real-world tools, and opens doors to long-term career growth in India and abroad.

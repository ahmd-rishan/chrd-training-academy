About Us

Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala) is an ISO 9001:2015 certified hub of qualified educators, professional trainers, and industry mentors.

Our Vision & Mission

Building Confidence Through Practical Skill Mastery

At CHRD Training Academy, we believe education must translate directly into career opportunities. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic theory and workplace demands across teacher training, digital marketing, healthcare admin, and corporate accounting.

Operating under CHRD Kerala, our ISO 9001:2015 certified curriculum ensures every student receives structured, high-quality, and job-ready instruction.

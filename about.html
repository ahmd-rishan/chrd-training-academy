About CHRD Training Academy

CHRD Training Academy is a skill development and professional training institution in Malappuram, Kerala, operating under the Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala). The academy focuses on practical, career oriented learning through teacher training, professional education, workshops and skill development programs.

Our Institution Story

About CHRD Training Academy

CHRD Training Academy provides practical and career focused learning opportunities for students, educators, job seekers and professionals. Through structured training programs, workshops and practical learning experiences, the institution aims to help learners develop relevant knowledge, professional skills and confidence.

Our ISO 9001:2015 certified standards reflect our commitment to structured educational governance, practical curriculum design, and continuous learner assessment. We combine academic rigor with live hands-on practice across Early Childhood Montessori Teacher Training, AI Digital Marketing, Hospital Administration, Legal Assistant training, and Office Secretarial skills.

At CHRD, education extends far beyond awarding certificates. We foster an encouraging, learner-centered environment where every individual builds genuine self-confidence, masters real-world tools, and opens doors to long-term career growth in India and abroad.

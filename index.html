About CHRD Kerala

Skill Development & Career Training

CHRD Training Academy operates under the Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala) - an ISO 9001:2015 certified hub of highly qualified educators, industry mentors, and professional trainers.

Established with a vision to provide accessible, high-quality, and career-focused education across Kerala, CHRD combines academic rigor with practical workforce demand. We believe education must not only award certificates, but build real-world confidence and unlock genuine career growth.

CHRD Training Academy works under the Centre for Human Resource Development Kerala (CHRD Kerala). CHRD Kerala is an ISO 9001:2015 certified Centre and a hub of well qualified teachers and trainers. As the Best Skill Development Academy in Malappuram, CHRD Training Academy is committed to helping individuals build meaningful careers through quality education and practical training.

Our programmes are designed for students, teachers, professionals, and aspiring job seekers who wish to strengthen their knowledge and develop real world skills.

With experienced trainers, learner focused teaching methods, and continuous support, we create an environment where every learner can grow with confidence.

At CHRD, we believe education should not only provide certificates but also create opportunities, build confidence, and prepare individuals for success in life and work.
